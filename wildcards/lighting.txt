# lighting v2 — one full lighting recipe per line (pattern + direction + quality + temperature).
# Confidence markers live ONLY in these group headers ([高]=learned danbooru tag, [中]=CLIP-parsable
# phrase, [低]=photo jargon carried by its inline plain-language description). Entries must not name
# environment objects; motivated light SOURCES (candle/bulb/lamp) are allowed as emitters only.
# A. named portrait patterns [低 — jargon; each line carries its own plain description]
rembrandt lighting, single warm key from upper left, small triangle of light on the shadowed cheek
split lighting, face half in shadow, hard vertical shadow edge down the nose
butterfly lighting, symmetrical key from high front, small soft shadow under the nose
loop lighting, key slightly off to one side, small nose shadow looping toward the cheek
short lighting, shadow side of the face turned to viewer, narrow dramatic portrait light
# B. edge / separation [高 — backlighting, rim light, silhouette are learned tags]
rim light, bright edge outline along hair and shoulders, face mostly in shadow
backlighting, contre-jour, glowing outline, details swallowed by shadow
silhouette, figure reading as a single dark shape against a bright field
kicker light from behind at forty-five degrees, one bright line along jaw and shoulder
hair light from high behind, halo glow on silver hair
# C. direction x quality [高/中]
sidelighting, hard raking light skimming across fabric texture, long shadows
harsh sunlight, hard-edged cast shadows, squinting brightness
soft diffused overcast light, shadowless gentle modelling
top-down spotlight, pool of light, eyes falling into shadow
gentle warm underlighting, face lit softly from below by a small flame
dappled sunlight, broken patches of light and shadow across the figure
# D. motivated practicals [中 — the filament-bulb key is print-validated (C4-505)]
warm candlelight, single flame as the only key light, deep shadow falloff
bare tungsten bulb glow, warm filament light, intimate amber cast
oil lamp glow, low warm pool of light, edges fading to darkness
paper lantern glow, soft warm omnidirectional light
cool blue moonlight, pale ambient wash, soft silver highlights
# E. color-temperature contrast / gels [中 — two-source color splits steer reliably]
warm orange key with cool blue fill, teal and orange split
duotone gel lighting, magenta key and cyan rim from opposite sides
cool moonlight ambient with a single warm accent light
golden hour backlight, long warm rays, cool blue shadow fill
neon glow spill, colored gel light washing over skin
volumetric lighting, god rays, visible shafts of light in haze
# F. value structure [高 — chiaroscuro validated best on Illustrious-XL-v2.0]
dramatic chiaroscuro, low-key, darkness swallowing most of the figure
low-key lighting, single small source, ninety percent shadow
high-key lighting, bright airy field, faint soft shadows only
a pool of light in darkness, figure emerging from black
warm indoor lamplight, cozy enveloping glow
lens flare, sun glare cutting across the frame
studio three-point lighting, clean key fill and rim
