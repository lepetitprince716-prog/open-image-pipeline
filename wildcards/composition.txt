# composition v2 — figure-vs-frame ONLY: where the figure sits in the frame and how the frame is
# divided. NO environment objects (background/props belong to the other line); the deliberately
# unspecified `blurry foreground` / `foreground silhouette framing` entries are the interface the
# object/background line may fill with concrete entities. Orthogonal to camera.txt (camera = where
# the camera is; composition = how the figure meets the frame). Known clashes with extreme camera
# picks are listed in wildcards/README.md — the sweep line absorbs them via eye-picking.
# A. placement [中 — symmetry is a learned tag]
rule of thirds, figure placed off-center
centered symmetrical composition, deliberate formal stillness
figure low in the frame, vast empty upper field
figure at the very edge of the frame, weighted empty space opposite
# B. negative space & scale [高 — negative space is a learned tag]
negative space, small figure in a wide empty field
figure filling the frame edge to edge, compressed and close
monumental emptiness, figure occupying a third of the frame height
tight framing, deliberate claustrophobic crop
# C. looking room / gaze space [中]
wide looking room, gazing across the frame into open space
back to viewer, facing into the empty side of the frame
gaze leading out of frame, unbalanced open space behind
# D. lines & diagonals [中 — vanishing point is a learned tag]
strong diagonal composition, body forming a rising diagonal
vanishing point perspective, figure standing at the convergence
vertical composition, tall unbroken standing line
zigzag composition, limbs breaking the frame into triangles
# E. depth layering [高 — depth of field / blurry foreground are learned tags]
blurry foreground, depth of field, figure sharp in the mid-ground
shallow depth of field, melting bokeh field behind the figure
foreground silhouette framing the figure, frame within a frame
layered depth, dark foreground band, lit middle ground
# F. figure-ground / silhouette readability [中]
strong silhouette readability, dark figure against a bright field
bright figure against a dark field, high figure-ground contrast
clothing reading as one bright graphic shape against darkness
partially out of frame, one shoulder cropped for tension
