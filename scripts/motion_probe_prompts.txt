# Motion probe candidates v1 — one prompt per line, '#' = comment.
# Usage: video_probe.py --image seed.png --prompts scripts/motion_probe_prompts.txt
# Same seed frame + same (content-derived) seed across all lines: the prompt is the
# only variable. First session: run a subset (baseline + 4-5 gestures + 1 camera +
# 1 zh mirror) rather than the whole file — ~2-4 min GPU per clip.
# Distill the survivors into wildcards/motion.txt once adherence verdicts exist.
#
# Community priors baked in (VIDEO_LINE_PLAN.md §3.5 research, 2026-07-02):
# - mechanically-specific active verbs beat passive ones; hand motion defaults to
#   "slow" unless the mechanics are spelled out — hence the paired wave prompts below
# - vertical 480x832 is motion-starved under the 4-step distill (lightx2v's own
#   thread) — probe gesture prompts with --res 832x480 as the H4 arm
# - the negative prompt is inert at cfg=1; only positive text steers

# -- baseline (the verified recipe's home turf; every probe run includes this)
gentle idle motion, hair swaying softly, slow blink

# -- precise single gestures (the adherence axis proper)
she raises her right hand
she tilts her head to the left
she slowly closes her eyes, then opens them
she waves her hand at the viewer
she lifts her right hand and waves at the viewer, wrist flexing softly
she brushes her hair behind her ear with one hand
she turns her head to look to the right
she smiles slightly
she crosses her arms

# -- body / larger motion (drift risk expected to rise with amplitude)
she takes one small step forward
she bows slightly
she turns around to face away

# -- camera only (subject stays still — separates camera adherence from body adherence)
camera slowly pushes in, she stays still
camera pans slightly to the right, she stays still

# -- physics
wind blows her hair and the hem of her coat

# -- zh mirrors (Wan is Alibaba-trained bilingual; test whether Chinese adheres better)
她抬起右手
她向左歪头
她缓缓眨了眨眼
镜头缓缓推近，人物保持不动
